For Grown-Ups 🌿

How to get the most out of Sprout (app + printables).

  • Keep it short & sweet. 5–10 minutes at a time is perfect for ages 2–4.
  • Narrate everything. Name colors, count out loud, repeat letter sounds.
  • Celebrate effort, not just answers. "You tried so hard!" beats "You're right!"
  • Follow their lead. Let your child re-do favorites — repetition is how they learn.
  • Pair screen + paper. Play "ABC Sounds" in the app, then trace those letters here.
  • Use the app's Parent Zone to switch the age level, slow the narration, or reduce motion.

A 5-Day Starter Rhythm

  • Mon: ABC Sounds + Letter tracing
  • Tue: Count With Me + Count & Trace page
  • Wed: Animal Safari + a coloring page
  • Thu: Story Time + talk about the lesson
  • Fri: Code a Path + the maze
